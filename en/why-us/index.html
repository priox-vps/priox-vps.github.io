Who actually does the work

He then hands over to a team that is put together from experienced freelancers for each project. In straightforward cases the work goes to that team directly. Team size is 5 to 10 people per project.

What that means for you: no working student sits on the phone, no rotating carousel works through a list. At the same time the founder does not make the calls himself on a permanent basis. For a sales service that has to scale, that would be the wrong promise. His role is to prepare and steer the project so that the quality of the conversations does not depend on a single person.

Everyone who calls brings years of their own B2B sales experience. They do not read out a script, they lead a conversation on substance. With a cold calling agency in particular that is not a detail: the work a simple call centre does not cover only begins after the second objection.

Onboarding in days, not months

The sequence refers to the example set in the section The trigger decides the call and is there for illustration. The sequence stays, the content is written anew for your market.

First the target group is narrowed down. Then we break the offer and its benefit into wording a decision maker understands without a technical lecture. After that we collect typical objections, review publicly visible triggers and define when a conversation does not fit.

Conversation management with evidence

A concrete trigger opens the door. After that we listen, clarify instead of countering, and steer the conversation with questions that make a situation visible. That way the pressure never comes from the caller, it comes from the client’s situation.

The foundations range from NLP and systemic conversation technique through facial-expression reading and social psychology to negotiation. What matters is translating them into a conversation that takes the other side seriously.

The voice carries the message

We are aware of the effect of our voice and do not sound like a run-of-the-mill salesperson. With downward inflection the voice falls at the end of the sentence instead of rising: „Then we speak on Tuesday at ten.“ becomes a calm statement rather than a request for permission. Chris Voss calls this slow delivery, falling at the end of the sentence, the „late-night FM DJ voice“; it lowers resistance before the content takes effect.

For a team that wants to build this craft itself, we combine the work with B2B sales training. In outsourced sales we put the same conversation quality into practice operationally. Both routes are described in the service overview.

Signal Based Selling

The trigger decides the call

If you have never come across the term: what is meant is a simple reversal. The usual approach is to work through a target list from top to bottom – who you reach is decided by the order of the list. We turn that around and wait for an event that becomes publicly visible at a company we want to work with. Only then do we call. The plain description is outreach based on a concrete occasion.

The difference is not a question of wording. With a list the moment is random, and random usually means: no current topic. With a trigger the company is already dealing with the very subject we want to talk about.

Our central sales intelligence system analyses millions of sources carrying relevant data. It recognises more than 40 types of trigger – and if it has to be 100, it is 100.

Signal Based Selling supplies the trigger; the conversation management turns it into a concrete test of whether an exchange makes sense.

A publicly visible signal is simply a concrete occasion – a posted vacancy, a new site or a product announcement – examples, not a closing list. “You posted two roles in Vienna last week. How are you organising the outreach for the new market?” is one possible opening. “Your new product line has been announced publicly. Who is responsible for making the first conversations happen?” is a second.

The events we use come exclusively from publicly accessible sources. In the conversation we name only the concrete occasion, never a pattern observed over a longer period. That keeps the opening comprehensible and stops it feeling like surveillance.

Frequently asked questions

Who actually makes the calls for us?

Every project is called by a team of experienced B2B freelancers from our network. Florian Weippert works his way into target clients, offer, objections and playbook personally and runs the handover. In straightforward cases the team starts directly. We do not use people briefed at short notice or an anonymous call centre.

Does the founder make the calls himself?

The founder does not make the calls himself on a permanent basis. His task is the onboarding, sharpening the conversation management and steering the playbook. That keeps the experience inside the project without your result depending on a single person. Who calls operationally is clearly defined as part of the project team before the start.

What makes you different from a call centre?

A call centre usually works with shifts, short briefings and a script. At DEALMAKERS people with their own B2B sales experience lead the conversations on substance. They can place an objection, ask a follow-up question and close cleanly. Before every call there is also a publicly visible trigger instead of a bought list.

You quote target attainment above 200 % – does that apply to our revenue?

No. The figures are personal target attainment from earlier employed sales roles and not a revenue forecast for your company. They document the context in which the conversation management was formed: quota pressure, real rejections and B2B sales that need explaining. What is agreed are traceable activities, clean conversations and meetings with decision makers.

Where do the figures on this page come from?

The figures come from the roles listed in the career record and from sales and training work carried out personally. They are not added-up client results of DEALMAKERS. That is why we place them in context on this page, instead of turning an earlier target attainment into a promise for a later assignment.

Do you also work in industries you do not yet know?

Yes, if the offer, the target client and the benefit are clear enough to make them concrete in a conversation. The range so far reaches from SaaS and fintech to telematics, construction and infrastructure, consulting and staffing. In the first few days we test language, objections and triggers. We do not take on a product without a clear benefit.

How long does onboarding take until the first conversations run?

Onboarding is designed in days, not months. First the target group, the offer and the exclusion criteria are set. Then follow the objection library, the trigger logic, the conversation opening and a joint review of the first cases. Only when these foundations hold does the outreach begin. A fixed start date is agreed per project.

Do you take on B2C calling without consent?

We do not take on B2C telephone advertising without consent. Our work is aimed at B2B conversations with a concrete, factually fitting trigger. Before every project we clarify the target group, the offer and the permissible scope of the conversation.

What happens if the agreed targets are not reached?

Then we do not argue with a figure from the past; the route is reviewed in a traceable way: target clients, triggers, reachability, how the conversations ran and the quality of the meetings. The playbook is documented, so it is possible to see where the assumption does not hold. We do not give a revenue guarantee; transparent work and a clean review we do.

How do we start?

In a first call we clarify the offer, the target clients, the sales capacity you already have and the question of whether external capacity is the right answer at all. If it fits, a kickoff follows with positioning, objections and first triggers. If it does not fit, we say so before the project starts. You can arrange a slot by phone, email or calendar.

Thirty minutes are enough for an honest review.

We clarify the offer, the target clients and the question of whether we are the right choice. Phone, email and calendar are open to you directly.
